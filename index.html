01 / About

International affairs, strategy, and business analysis grounded in experience.

I bring ten years of public-sector and international affairs experience, complemented by applied training in Business Analysis and Data Analytics.

I am a public and international affairs professional with a background in international relations and public administration, complemented by capabilities in business analysis and data analytics. My experience spans foreign policy, public policy, government and legislative relations, strategic planning, institutional coordination, stakeholder management, performance monitoring, and executive reporting.

During more than seven years in the Office of Mexico's Foreign Minister, I progressed from advisor to deputy director and director, and served for six months as Acting Director General of Programs and Institutional Information. I coordinated inputs from more than 15 internal areas, liaised with the Presidency, the Federal Public Administration, and Congress, including the Senate, and translated complex institutional information into executive briefs and actionable recommendations for four Foreign Ministers. My work also included coordinating the consolidation of reports and work programs for more than 160 Mexican missions abroad, as well as integrating performance information from 27 responsible units for a budget program's Results Indicator Matrix (MIR).

I have complemented this experience by completing TripleTen's Data Analyst Bootcamp and pursuing IBM's Business Analyst Professional Certificate. My recent training and portfolio work use SQL, Python, Excel, Tableau, and process modeling to analyze performance, document requirements, map workflows, and develop practical, evidence-based recommendations.
