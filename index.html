01 / About

Public & international affairs, complemented by business analysis.

A decade of public-sector experience, complemented by analytical and business analysis capabilities.

I am a public and international affairs professional with a background in international relations and public administration. My career spans Mexico's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Government of the State of Mexico, and the Embassy of Mexico in Brazil, with experience in foreign policy, government and legislative relations, strategic planning, institutional coordination, and stakeholder management.

At the Ministry, I spent more than seven years in the Office of the Chief of Staff to the Secretary of Foreign Affairs, progressing from Advisor to Director and serving concurrently as Acting Director General for six months. My work involved engaging senior government and legislative counterparts and coordinating inputs across institutional teams.

What sets me apart is the combination of senior-level strategic coordination and priority management with a strong analytical component and hands-on experience in Business Analysis and Data Analytics. I completed TripleTen's Data Analyst Bootcamp and am currently pursuing IBM's Business Analyst Professional Certificate, combining practical data tools such as SQL, Python, Excel, and Tableau with requirements analysis, process modeling, stakeholder collaboration, and solution evaluation.
