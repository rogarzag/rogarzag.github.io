01 / About

Experience across public affairs, international relations, and business analysis.

A career spanning foreign policy, government relations, institutional coordination, and analytical work.

I am a public and international affairs professional with degrees in International Relations and Public Administration. My experience spans Mexico's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Government of the State of Mexico, and the Embassy of Mexico in Brazil, covering foreign policy, government relations, international cooperation, and security affairs.

At Mexico's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, I spent more than seven years in the Office of the Chief of Staff, progressing from Advisor to Director and serving as Acting Director General for six months. In these roles, I coordinated work across more than 15 internal areas, contributed to foreign affairs priorities in two National Development Plans and two Sectoral Programs, and coordinated the Ministry's six-year accountability report. My work also included international analysis, decision-support materials, and liaison with the Technical Secretariat of the National Security Council and the National Intelligence Center, contributing to a national security program and report.

I complement this experience with applied Business Analysis and Data Analysis capabilities. Andes Capital — Real Estate Analysis

Analyzed 8,500 sales across 2023–2024, covering $6.01B in revenue, 11.14% year-over-year growth, property types, sales channels, and customer cohorts.

Findings & recommendations: brokers generated approximately 73% of revenue and First-Time Buyers 63%; initial cohort sales fell from 224 in January 2023 to 26 in December 2024. Recommended monitoring broker and direct-channel performance, tracking first-time and repeat buyers, and distinguishing revenue, average ticket, and volume by property type.

Andes Retail — Commercial Performance

Compared 2024–2025 revenue, margin, orders, customer segments, product categories, geography, and seasonality. Revenue declined 6.8%, alongside lower order volume and average order value.
