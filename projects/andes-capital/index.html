Real Estate Commercial Performance and Customer Cohort Analysis

Business objective

Evaluate commercial performance and identify the sales, channel, customer, property and time patterns associated with the results. The project presents three complementary views: Executive Overview, Commercial Analysis and Customer Cohort Analysis.

Customer cohort analysis

Recommendations based on the findings

  • Monitor Broker channel performance and compare its revenue trend with Direct sales, given the high share of revenue from brokers.
  • Distinguish revenue, average ticket and sales volume when setting priorities by property type.
  • Monitor First-Time Buyers alongside repeat buyers when reviewing the sales mix.
  • Compare initial cohort sales separately from repeat purchases; lower initial counts alone do not demonstrate lower retention.
  • Use recurring monthly peaks as a reference for planning, while checking whether the pattern continues.
