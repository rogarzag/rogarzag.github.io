MercadoLibre — Funnel & Retention Analysis

E-commerce Conversion Funnel and User Retention

Used SQL to examine the e-commerce conversion funnel and D7, D14, D21 and D28 retention across ten Latin American countries, comparing markets and customer cohorts.

Project repository ↗ Analysis workbook ↗

Business questions

  • At which stage does the largest conversion drop occur?
  • Which countries have the strongest and weakest add-to-cart performance?
  • How does retention change between D7, D14, D21 and D28?
  • Which countries and cohorts show comparatively stronger long-term retention?
  • What actions could improve conversion and user engagement?

Methodology

  • Organized user events by funnel stage and calculated the percentage of users reaching each stage.
  • Compared funnel performance across ten Latin American countries.
  • Grouped users into cohorts according to their first activity date.
  • Measured retention at D7, D14, D21 and D28.
  • Validated results and interpreted the main business opportunities.

Business recommendations

  • Investigate the transition from product selection to cart addition, including calls to action, product information, pricing, availability and possible usability barriers.
  • Prioritize early-retention actions between D7 and D14, such as relevant reminders, personalized follow-up and re-engagement experiments.
  • Compare stronger country results with lower-performing markets to identify practices worth testing, without assuming that market differences have a single cause.
