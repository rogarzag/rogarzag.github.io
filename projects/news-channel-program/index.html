News Channel Program — UML Use Case Modeling

Use Case Modeling for a Live News Program

Defined the functional scope of a prime-time news program by identifying the people and supporting roles that interact with it, documenting the main system functions, and mapping actor-to-use-case associations in UML.

Business context and system scope

The modeled system is News Channel Program. Its high-level scope covers preparation and delivery of news content, the live broadcast, audience interaction, broadcast equipment support and advertisement placement.

Actors and participation

ActorRole in the modeled system
News producerPlans segments, selects topics, assigns reporters and coordinates live coverage.
ReporterGathers information, conducts interviews, contributes stories and reports live.
ViewerWatches the broadcast and shares comments or feedback through social media.
Technical teamSets up, maintains and troubleshoots broadcast equipment.
AdvertiserSubmits advertisements and coordinates their placement with the channel.

Use cases

Modeling approach

  • Defined the system boundary before identifying the actors and use cases.
  • Separated participants from the functions they interact with.
  • Documented the eight actor-to-use-case associations to clarify participation.
  • Kept the model at a high level rather than introducing detailed workflows or technical architecture.

The diagram uses actor associations only. It does not introduce include or extend relationships because the exercise does not define use cases that require them.

What this model supports

Use case modeling makes the system scope and participant interactions visible before more detailed requirements are gathered. It provides a starting point for clarifying functional requirements across editorial, technical, audience and commercial activities.
