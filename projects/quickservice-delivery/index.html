QuickService Delivery — Process Mapping

Food and Grocery Order Management Workflow

Mapped the order journey for a fictional food and grocery delivery service, from customer order placement to pickup or confirmed home delivery, clarifying responsibilities and process controls.

Business context and process scope

The process starts when a customer submits an order and provides the required details. It ends when the customer collects the order or receipt of the home delivery is confirmed.

Process model

Analysis and decision logic

  • Order completeness: Customer Service requests missing information and validates the order again.
  • Payment receipt: Sales follows up with the customer and verifies payment again after submission or retry.
  • Order readiness: Production completes preparation or resolves the issue before checking readiness again.
  • Fulfillment method: Warehouse follows the customer's pickup or home-delivery preference.

What the model makes visible

  • Handoffs and responsibilities across five participants.
  • Control points that determine whether an order can advance.
  • Alternative fulfillment paths for pickup and home delivery.
  • Exception paths for incomplete information, payment follow-up and preparation issues.

The exception paths are explicit modeling assumptions. The course scenario does not define a payment cancellation policy or detailed exception-handling rules, so the model uses follow-up and rechecking rather than assuming an unsupported cancellation outcome.
